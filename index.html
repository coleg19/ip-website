How to Make a Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup boiling water

Steps

  1. Prep the oven: Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Mix dry ingredients: Whisk sugar, flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. Add wet ingredients: Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract. Beat on medium speed with a hand mixer for about 2 minutes until smooth.
  4. Thin the batter: Stir in the boiling water by hand. The batter will be very thin and watery, but this is the secret to its ultra-moist texture.
  5. Bake: Pour evenly into your pans and bake for 30 to 35 minutes. A toothpick inserted in the center should come out clean.
  6. Cool: Let them cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
